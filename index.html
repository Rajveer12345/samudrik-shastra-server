✦ Why Right Hand? ✦
In Vedic palmistry, the right hand is the active hand — it represents the life you are actively living, creating and earning through your karma in this lifetime.

The right hand shows your current destiny, career, relationships and unfolding events. This is why we always read the right hand — it gives the most accurate and current reading regardless of gender.

Best results: palm facing up, fingers relaxed, good natural light.
