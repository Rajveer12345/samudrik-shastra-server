✦ Why Right Hand? ✦
In Vedic palmistry, the right hand is the active hand — it represents the life you are actively creating through your choices, actions, and karma earned in this lifetime.

The right hand shows what you are doing with what you were given — your current destiny, career path, relationships, and the events unfolding in your life right now.

This is why we always read the right hand — it gives the most accurate and current reading regardless of gender. The left hand shows inherited tendencies from birth, which is secondary information.

For best results: palm facing up, fingers relaxed, natural position. Good lighting.
